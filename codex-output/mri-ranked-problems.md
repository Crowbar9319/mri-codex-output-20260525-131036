## Terms Used

- **Central canal stenosis**: narrowing of the main passage through which the lower spinal nerve bundle travels.
- **Neural foraminal narrowing**: narrowing of the openings where nerves leave the spine.
- **Traversing nerve root**: a nerve passing downward inside the canal toward a lower exit point.
- **Annular tear**: a tear in the outer ring of a spinal disc.
- **Disc desiccation**: loss of water content in a disc, often described as a degenerative or wear-related change.
