# Questions for a Spine Specialist or Doctor

1. How concerning is the `L4-L5` moderate-to-severe central canal stenosis and reported aggregation of cauda equina fibers in the context of my symptoms and neurological examination?
2. Are my pain, numbness, tingling, weakness, or walking limitations consistent with the nerve roots described as pressured or touched at `L3-L4`, `L4-L5`, or `L5-S1`?
3. What symptoms would mean I need same-day or emergency evaluation, particularly for cauda equina nerve problems?
4. Do the two report entries represent different MRI dates, and if so, is there demonstrable progression compared with the earlier images?
5. Should the original MRI images be reviewed directly to confirm the degree and location of nerve compression described in the report?
6. What physical examination findings or additional tests would help determine which MRI changes matter clinically?
7. What are the reasonable management options for my particular symptoms, and what would trigger reconsideration if symptoms worsen?
